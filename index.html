Who we are

Delmix provides a wide range of products and services for the building material industry, with an all-round service that runs from the first concept to the plant that is still producing years later.

Delmix Engineering is the Australian distributor for NICHI, recognised across the industry for the high quality equipment it markets under the BUFFALO brand. Since 1981 NICHI has provided expertise in mechanical and hydraulic engineering for the concrete batching industry.
