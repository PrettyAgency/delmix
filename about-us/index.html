About us

Since 1981 NICHI has provided expertise in mechanical and hydraulic engineering for the concrete batching industry. Delmix Engineering is the Australian distributor for NICHI products.

Nichi is the leading supplier of concrete mixers and batching plants in South East Asia, and continues to further research and develop products to meet the ever changing demands in the market and the environment.

Nichi is recognised in the industry for its ability to deliver the high quality equipment marketed under the BUFFALO brand.

Founded by Malaysian concrete industry icon Jimmy Nichi with the aim of delivering quality engineered products to the heavy building materials industry, Nichi is the leading manufacturer and service centre for batching plants, concrete mixers and building material related products and services in Malaysia, South East Asia and the Pacific. We are always looking for ways to improve our operations and production methods.
