Why dry mix mortar

These mortars are essential for the aesthetic finishing of a building and its thermal insulation, and essential in increasing construction efficiency.

Dry mix mortars are construction materials mainly comprising cement, sands and chemical additives, precisely weighed and mixed in a factory. This mix is termed mortar and is packed in bags.

Several types of mortars can be formulated in a dry mix plant. Dry mix mortars offer the perfect solution when consistent quality, controlled inventory, time and speed are important aspects of your project.
