Mobile concrete batch plant

Our Mobile Concrete Batch Plant is suitable for temporary installation, special projects or permanent facilities. The mobile concrete batch plants by Delmix provide a cost-effective and reliable solution, with the advantage of being deployed in just a matter of days. Whenever space limitations, fast set-up or easy moving are concerned, a BUFFALO mobile plant is the optimal solution.

Production capacity
Between 40 m³ and 60 m³ per hour
Standard models
Ramp fed type and ground bin type
Expansion
Capacity can be increased by adding auxiliaries such as silos and storage bins
Compliance
ADR compliant, fitted with a National Heavy Vehicle Regulator approved compliance plate

Advantages of a BUFFALO mobile dry batch plant

  • Low profile and high mobility batch plant
  • Quick and easy set-up
  • Affordable and favours mid-range projects
  • Minimal set up space required
  • Easy and inexpensive to transport
  • Requires less foundation (70 to 80% less than stationary concrete batching plants)
  • Heavy duty frame
  • Factory wired, plumbed and tested
  • Minimises concrete transportation cost

Mobile Batch Plant MBP40, wet or dry batch

Mobilise quickly. Maximise your investment.

Trailer-mounted and towed by standard prime movers. Operational within hours, with no cranes and no foundations.

40–110m³/hr

Adjustable output to meet demanding schedules.

  • Wet-mix (with twin-shaft mixer) or dry-mix options for civil, structural or precast concrete
  • Integrated cement weigh hopper, water system and admixture dosing
  • Compatible with Delmix control systems or your choice of batch control system
  • Australian-engineered for harsh, remote conditions: heat, dust and heavy-duty use
  • Designed and built to Australian transport, engineering and safety standards
  • Easily moved between sites, maximising utilisation and avoiding stranded assets
