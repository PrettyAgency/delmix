The barrel and blades

An efficient and homogenous mix, guaranteed

SS400 high tensile, abrasion resistant steel is the preferred choice for both the barrel and blades as it meets the JIS and AS4100 standards. SS400 is highly regarded for its tested durability to withstand the most demanding agitating conditions.

Our computer aided precision design and spiral configuration on the mixing blades ensures you get the homogenous mix required with every batch you make. These hot rolled blades are fabricated with precise uniformity to allow efficient mixing quality.

01

Finless blades

The most prominent feature: our blades are finless, uniquely patterned compared to the common type.

02

Homogenous mixing

The blade configuration has proven to accommodate excellent homogenous mixing.

03

Less build-up

Minimises residual build-up, unlike conventional blades.

04

Longer life span

A protective layer welded on the blade ends provides a longer life span.
