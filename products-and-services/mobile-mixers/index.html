Mobile twin shaft mixer / mobile wet mix

The BUFFALO Mobile Twin Shaft Mixer (BMTS) joined the BUFFALO product line in 2018. Its unique design can transform your existing dry batch concrete plant into a wet batch plant with minimal modifications to your existing batching structure.

Low profile, any orientation

Used together with our Mobile Dry Batch Plant, the BMTS gives a low profile design and can be orientated at any angle to the centreline of the plant to suit your site layout.

The BMTS

Built around fast truck charging

  • Wide conveyor belt which allows for fast truck charging
  • Customisable discharge height
  • Fast installation
  • Easily transportable
  • Easy access for cleaning, inspection, maintenance and service
  • Adjustable discharge gate
  • Heavy duty 3-axle trailer with adjustable legs
  • Low profile allows set up under a typical dry concrete batch plant, with minor modifications to the discharge section
