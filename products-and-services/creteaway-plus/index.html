How it works

Apply, let it work, rinse away

The label says it in four words: spray on, rinse off. The steps below follow the UK manufacturer's guidance.

  1. Apply

    Spray or brush CreteAway+ directly onto dried concrete. It is designed to cling to the surface, including vertical faces and hard-to-reach areas.

  2. Let it work

    Leave it to penetrate the build-up. The manufacturer's guide is typically 10 to 20 minutes, depending on thickness.

  3. Rinse away

    Wash the loosened residue off with water and get the truck or plant back to work.

Application method and timing will vary with your equipment and the amount of build-up. The Delmix team will confirm the right approach for your site at an on-site demonstration.
