Questions

Before you switch

What is CreteAway+?

CreteAway+ is a premium concrete removal system for concrete producers. It removes concrete build-up from trucks and plant with a low corrosive, biodegradable formula.

How is it different from hydrochloric acid cleaners?

It is low corrosive and safer to handle, with very low fume and exposure risk. That means less corrosion and chemical attack on your trucks, and a safer wash bay for your crew.

Where is it made?

CreteAway+ was developed in the United Kingdom and is now produced under licence in South Australia for the Australian market.
